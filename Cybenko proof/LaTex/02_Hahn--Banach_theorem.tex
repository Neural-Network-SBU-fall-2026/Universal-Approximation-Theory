\section*{Hahn--Banach Theorem (Normed Version)}

\subsection*{Formal Statement}

Let:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \( X \) be a real normed vector space.
    \item \( M \subseteq X \) be a linear subspace.
    \item \( f : M \to \mathbb{R} \) be a bounded linear functional on \( M \).
\end{itemize}

Then there exists a bounded linear functional
\[
F : X \to \mathbb{R}
\]
such that:
\[
F(x) = f(x) \quad \forall x \in M
\]
and
\[
\| F \| = \| f \|
\]
Therefore, \( F \) is an extension of \( f \) to the entire space \( X \), without increasing its norm.
